[INTRO - SOUND SYSTEM]

Mlinzi hajasoma jina lake bado.

[VERSE 1 - DUB DELIVERY]
[Spoken Word - Heavy Delay]
Zawadi anaweka bakuli lake chini ya bomba,

"Nuru hayupo kwenye orodha ya leo."
(Cello swells in, rhodes chord fades)
Papasi wanasaga karatasi ya reli,
Mlinzi anafunga mlango wa chuma kwa funguo mbili.

(Snare rolls enter, filtered reese rises)

[VERSE 2 - DUB DELIVERY]
[Spoken Word - Close Mic]

"Kila kitu kinachofungwa, kinataka kufunguliwa."
(Rhodes stab, cello pizzicato)
