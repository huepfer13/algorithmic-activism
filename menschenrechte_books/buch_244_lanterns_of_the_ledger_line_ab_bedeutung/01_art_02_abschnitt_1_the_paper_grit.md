# Abschnitt 1: The Paper Grit

Style of Music: Afrobeat with analog modular synths, 76 BPM, wall of sound · low end: sub pulse below 50 Hz · mid: close-mic lead vocal · wide: pads spread wide · polyrhythmic percussion, tape-saturated drums, glassy arpeggios, spoken-word verses, half-time chorus, -no acoustic ballad, -no lo-fi chill, -no slow tempo
Mood: procedural, tender, haunted

[Intro]
(Sub pulse enters, paper rustle loop)
The tube line choked on Monday's dust—
a fine white grit on every relay's trust.

[Verse 1]
Mara Voss holds the printout by its edge,
the ninth-floor lamp still burning at her desk.
The pneumatic chute coughs once and goes dead,
a paper-slurry clogging up the thread.
She wipes the contact clean with one bare thumb,
the grit is fine as flour, pale and numb.
The numbers on the sheet won't reconcile—
a surname flagged, a column out of style.

[Pre-Chorus]
(Clock ticks, bass climbs)
The housing list came back today,
one family's name just washed away.
A foreign syllable, a hyphened line—
the form said no, the form was fine.

[Chorus]
(Drums hit half-time, pads bloom wide)
Paper grit on the relay line,
the chute won't carry what they signed.
Paper grit on the relay line,
the door stays shut, the form is fine.
(Paper grit—)
Paper grit on the relay line.

[Drop]
(Percussion resets to sub pulse and clave; one relay clicks open, then jams.)

[Verse 2]
Ilyan Brecht walks Twelve with empty hands,
the corridor is dark, the doorbell stands.
He knows the family's name—it's on his route,
the husband's coat, the daughter's quiet boot.
He knocks the code, the silence answers back,
the relay eats the slip, the tube goes slack.
He feels the grit between his fingers spread—
the chute is blocked, the message never read.

[Pre-Chorus]
(Whispered vocals, tape hiss)
The landlord signed the ledger clean,
the local names went through the screen.
The hyphen and the foreign sound—
the form said no, the form was sound.

[Chorus]
(Full wall of sound, group shout on hook)
Paper grit on the relay line,
the chute won't carry what they signed.
Paper grit on the relay line,
the door stays shut, the form is fine.
(Paper grit—)
Paper grit on the relay line.

[Bridge - stripped, vocal-forward, falsetto]
Sable Odum reads the column twice,
the flag's not random—it's precise.
The mechanism's calibrated to exclude,
a quiet filter built into the mood.
Tobin Reyes hums the family's name,
the syllables a small and stubborn flame.
The Adjuster marks the page in red—
the chute is blocked, the words unsaid.

[Outro]
(Dub outro, heavy delay on the hook)
The relay jams, the paper falls—
a door unopened, a silent hall.
The grit is fine, the form is signed.
(Paper grit on the relay line.)
