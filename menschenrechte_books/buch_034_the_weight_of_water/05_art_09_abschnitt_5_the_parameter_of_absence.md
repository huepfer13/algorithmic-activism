# Abschnitt 5: The Parameter of Absence

The whine cut through the chamber at forty-seven hertz — the same frequency as the lamps' flicker, Mara noted, the same count she had held in her fingers when the ozone bit. The intercoms had been singing it for three hours now, a damping field's complaint about something moving in the deep rock. Teo's jaw tightened. He did not look at the stranger.

"The archive calls it *unbound-variable*," Juniper said. Her stylus did not hover this time. It pressed. "The condition where a subject occupies space without occupying a row. The well's logic cannot close the loop. The count requires closure."

Old Sable's rope creaked. The stranger had not moved from the lamplight's edge — dry, clear, unblinking. Dust on the contacts had not moved. But the whine shifted, dropped to thirty-two hertz, and the stranger's throat worked once. A swallow. The sound of a body that remembered water.

"Exile is a parameter," Juniper continued, reading from the forty-ninth row where her hand had written without her consent. "Detention is a parameter. The archive distinguishes them by duration. The archive does not distinguish them by *cause*."

Teo's bucket hung empty. "And the body?"

"The body is the remainder," Juniper said. "The calculation that remains when the ledger balances. Protection of integrity precedes the count. The archive wrote that in the first row. The archive has spent forty-nine rows forgetting it."

The whine spiked — sixty hertz, sharp as a needle in the eardrum. Mara's hand went to her pocket where the tally-stick was not. The grain remembered. The stranger's eyes found hers across the lamplight, and in them she saw the well's own reflection: black, patient, waiting for the rope to pull.

Old Sable's lips moved. Soundless. The snake's coils tightened.

"The stranger drinks," she said. "The stranger is measured. The measurement enters the count. But the *absence* — the space where a name should be — that parameter has no row."

Juniper's stylus broke. The snap echoed in the sudden silence after the whine died.

The stranger took one step forward. The dust on the contacts stirred.
