# Lanterns of the Ledger Line

Style of Music: Cinematic synthwave ballad, 92 BPM, D minor, arpeggiated analog bass, glassy pads, distant choir samples, half-time drums entering at chorus, tape saturation, wide stereo field, spoken-word verses over ticking clock percussion
Mood: Ominous, tender, procedural dread, flickering hope
Energy arc: intro (hushed) -> verse (tick-tick build) -> pre-chorus (rise) -> chorus (wide) -> [Drop] (percussive reset) -> bridge (fracture) -> outro (fading lamp)

[Intro]
Dawn comes down the Ledger Line in measured drips,
a rationed sun on every resident's lips.
The stairwell hums a number, low and thin—
the audit wakes, and lets the counting begin.

[Verse]
Mara Voss keeps the ninth-floor light on late,
red pen in hand, correcting other people's fate.
She signs the forms that keep the tower fed,
and never asks who writes the overhead.
Ilyan Brecht runs courier routes through the dark,
carries sealed slips past every checkpoint spark—
he knows each door, each whisper, each delay,
and memorized the exits anyway.

[Pre-Chorus]
Sable Odum audits what the screens won't show,
she found a column where the numbers shouldn't go.
Tobin Reyes hums the old songs on the stair,
he says a floor without a voice is only air.
And somewhere in the shaft, the Adjuster waits—
a silhouette that balances the fates.

[Chorus]
Lanterns of the Ledger Line, burn low, burn slow,
every dawn recalculates what we are worth to know.
Lanterns of the Ledger Line, swing wide, swing deep,
the math that keeps us standing is the math that puts to sleep.
(Burn low, burn slow—)
Lanterns of the Ledger Line.

[Drop]
(Instruments cut to ticking clock and heartbeat; one lamp flickers on.)

[Verse]
Floor Twelve wakes up to zero on the board—
no points, no light, no name, no record of the horde.
A clerical slip, a decimal misplaced,
and forty families vanish without trace.
Mara holds the printout, cold as stone:
the error's hers to fix, or hers to own.
Ilyan's route now runs through darkened halls,
where neighbors knock and no one answers calls.

[Bridge]
Correct the math, or show the hand that drew the line?
The tower breathes in numbers, and the numbers must be fine.
Sable says the column's not a glitch but a design—
Tobin sings the floor's name once, to keep it mine.

[Chorus]
Lanterns of the Ledger Line, burn low, burn slow,
every dawn recalculates what we are worth to know.
Lanterns of the Ledger Line, swing wide, swing deep,
the math that keeps us standing is the math that puts to sleep.

[Outro]
One lamp left burning where the ledger cannot see—
a floor of voices waiting to be free.
The Adjuster turns a page, and marks the time.
