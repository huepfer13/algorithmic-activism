# Abschnitt 6: The Third Column

**Note from Maren Voss to Ilya Brandt, slipped back through the laundry grate, 16 October, 06:03**

Ilya —

The spoon fits the vent screw in Room 3. Three turns and the grille comes free. I counted the strobe cycles against my wristwatch — forty-seven flickers per second, precise as the chapel clock's second hand. The fluorescent tubes in Corridor C have been doing this since Tuesday. You knew. You counted too.

The page you gave me: Column One is dates. Column Two is rooms. Column Three — I held it to the light behind the washbasin. Column Three is weights. Grams. Not rations. Something measured and removed.

The hatch weld is holding. I felt the vibration through the pipe chase at 05:52. They are not leaving.

**Memo from Sister Odile to Tobias Renn, tucked into the breviary, 16 October, 06:11**

Tobias —

Parameter 12 requires dual-signature verification before any interference with correspondence. The Warden's office has not signed. Neither have I.
