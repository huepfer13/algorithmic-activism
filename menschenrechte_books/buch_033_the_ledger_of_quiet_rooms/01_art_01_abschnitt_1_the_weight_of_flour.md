# Abschnitt 1: The Weight of Flour

**Memo from Sister Odile to the Warden, 15 October, 06:15**

Warden,

The flour scale is off by eleven grams. I have weighed it three times against the brass standard from the chapel and three times it reads eleven grams light. I am telling you this because you will hear it from someone else if I do not, and I would rather you hear it from me with the numbers attached.

The pneumatic tube to your office is blocked again. I sent the morning requisition through at five and heard it lodge somewhere in the wall near the kitchen corridor. There is paper dust on the relay contacts — I wiped them with my sleeve and they are grey again within the hour. Whatever is grinding up there is grinding fine. You should have someone look at it before the requisitions stop moving entirely.

I am not accusing anyone of the flour. I am telling you the scale is light and I have signed for every ounce that passed through my hands, and I will answer for it. If the scale is wrong, then my signature is wrong, and I do not intend to be wrong.

Send someone to fix the tube.

— Sister Odile

**Note from Tobias Renn to Ilya Brandt, passed under the door of Room 4, 15 October**

Ilya —

You counted thirty-one spoons at breakfast. There are thirty-two. I counted twice because I thought I had made the error, and then I counted a third time and it was still thirty-two, and you had already written thirty-one in your book and closed it.

I am not telling you this to correct you. I am telling you because you said a ledger that is not spoken can be altered, and I have been thinking about what happens when a ledger that *is* spoken is altered anyway. You said the number out loud. Everyone heard it. And now the number is wrong and it is in your hand and no one will remember that the spoon was there.

I have started keeping my own count. I am sorry. I did not want to. But I watched you write thirty-one and I thought: if I say nothing, then I am the one who let it be thirty-one.

The Warden walked past at three. He stopped outside your door for a long time. I could hear his pen.

— T.

**File entry, Warden's office, 15 October, 07:50**

Guest Brandt, I. — verbal count at breakfast recorded as thirty-one implements. Physical inventory at 07:30 records thirty-two. Discrepancy: one unit. Guest Renn, T. — observed counting independently, twice, lips moving. Note: guest Renn's count matched inventory. Guest Brandt's did not. Recommend separate observation of both guests at next meal. Recommend calibration of kitchen scale and inspection of pneumatic line. Paper dust on relay contacts noted by staff. Cause unknown.

Protection of physical integrity requires that no guest be permitted to believe a false count. Protection of life requires that no guest be permitted to believe a true one unverified. Both guests will be weighed against the standard at noon.
