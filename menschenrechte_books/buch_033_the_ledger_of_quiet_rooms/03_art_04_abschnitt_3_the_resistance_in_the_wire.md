# Abschnitt 3: The Resistance in the Wire

**Memo from Sister Odile to the Warden, 15 October, 16:03**

Warden,

The fluorescent tubes in Corridor C have begun to strobe at 47 Hz. I counted the cycles against the second hand of the chapel clock — forty-seven flickers per second, precise as a metronome. The ozone smell at the switch cabinets is sharp enough to taste. Copper and something older. The pneumatic relays are not the only thing grinding.

I have replaced the ballast in fixture three. The strobe persisted. I have checked the feed voltage — 230.4 V, stable. The anomaly is not in the supply. It is in the load. Something downstream is drawing current in pulses. Forty-seven times per second. The same frequency the mainland lab reported in the dust sample from the relay contacts, though they called it *acoustic resonance in the particulate matrix* and I call it a machine trying to speak.

Parameter 4.1 — *no guest shall be held in a state of uncompensated labor* — requires verification at 18:00 today. The kitchen scale logs show Brandt, I. performed 340 grams of peeling work at midday. The caloric ledger credits 12 kcal. The metabolic ledger debits 18 kcal. The deficit is 6 kcal. Over six weeks of storm isolation, that deficit becomes a debt the body pays in tissue.

I have noted the second ledger. Maren's hand on the page slipped under Room 4. *One for them. One for us.* I have not reported it. The audit arrives on the 22nd if the sea permits. The sea does not permit.

**Note from Tobias Renn to Maren Voss, folded into the hem of a laundry bag, 15 October, 16:41**

Maren —

The Warden's pen moved at Ilya's door. I saw it from the laundry window. Three strokes. Not a number. A symbol. Triangle with a line through the base. The alchemical sign for *sulfur*. The same mark appears on the maintenance ledger, page 44, line 3 — Sister Odile's correction weight entry.

The spoons were not miscounted. The thirty-second spoon is the one Ilya palmed when the Warden turned his back. It is in his mattress now, wrapped in the page he tore from his ledger. The page with the second column.

He is not hiding it from the audit. He is hiding it from the *calibration*.

**File entry, Warden's office, 15 October, 17:58**

Guest Brandt, I. — 18:00 re-weigh completed under witness (Sister Odile). Mass: 68.3 kg. Baseline: 68.4 kg. Deviation: -0.1 kg. Deviation now matches predicted metabolic loss. Parameter 4.1 verification: kitchen labor log cross-referenced with caloric ledger. Deficit 6 kcal sustained. Classification: *uncompensated labor threshold not breached*. However — pneumatic relay dust analysis returned from mainland lab via emergency drone drop at 17:30 before total comms failure. Composition: 12% copper, 3% zinc, 84% organic keratinized epithelium. Human skin. The walls are breathing the guests.

Protection of life requires that the 47 Hz strobe be traced to source before 06:00. Protection of physical integrity requires that the keratin finding be logged in all guest files before auditor arrival. Continuous observation protocol upgraded: audio monitoring activated in Rooms 1–5. Pneumatic tubes to be used for calibration data only. No guest correspondence permitted until audit clearance.
