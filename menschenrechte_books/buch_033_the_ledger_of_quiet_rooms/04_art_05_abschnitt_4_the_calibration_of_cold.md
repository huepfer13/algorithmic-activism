# Abschnitt 4: The Calibration of Cold

**Memo from Sister Odile to the Warden, 16 October, 03:12**

Warden,

The raised floor in Corridor C has begun to weep. Thermal drift in the plenum space — 14.2°C at 02:00, 13.8°C at 02:47, 13.1°C at 03:05. Condensate drops on the cable trays at 1.3-second intervals. I counted forty-six drops while the fluorescent tubes strobed their forty-seven cycles. The rhythm is not synchronized. The drift is not stabilized.

Parameter 5.1 — *no guest shall be held in a state of uncompensated sensory degradation* — requires verification at 06:00. The audio monitors in Rooms 1–5 recorded seventeen hours of breathing patterns. Room 4 (Voss, M.) shows 0.4 Hz variance in respiratory rate during the strobe cycles. Room 2 (Brandt, I.) shows 0.0 Hz variance. He is not breathing with the walls. He is listening to them.

I remember the pen at his door. Three strokes. Sulfur. The laundry window frames the memory: the Warden's hand, the symbol, the silence after. Tobias's note found me in the chapel at 18:41, folded into a cassock hem. *He is hiding it from the calibration.* The spoon. The page. The second column.

The keratin finding has been logged in all five guest files. The auditor's drone will not arrive. The sea does not permit. The condensate continues to fall on the cable trays, and the pneumatic relays grind, and the 47 Hz strobe persists in Corridor C while the thermal drift exceeds the 15°C threshold for Parameter 5.1 compliance.

**Note from Maren Voss to Ilya Brandt, pressed into the gap beneath Room 4's door, 16 October, 03:47**

Ilya —

The dripping wakes me. One point three seconds. Forty-six drops per minute. The walls are breathing us. I have the page. The second column. *One for them. One for us.* The spoon is still in your mattress. The Warden's audio feeds run through the same cable trays the condensate is eating. If the insulation fails, the calibration fails. If the calibration fails, Parameter 5.1 triggers —
