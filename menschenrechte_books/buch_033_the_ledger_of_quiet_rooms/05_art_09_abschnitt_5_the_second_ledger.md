# Abschnitt 5: The Second Ledger

**Note from Ilya Brandt to Maren Voss, slipped beneath the laundry grate, 16 October, 05:22**

Maren —

The hatch is welded. I heard the grinder at 04:40 through the pipe chase, three floors down, and the smell of hot metal came up through the drain. They are sealing us in before they seal themselves out. Listen: the audio trunk runs past my wall. The condensate has reached the insulation. When it crosses, the feed dies, and the Warden will have no ears in Corridor C. That is our window.

You have the page. I have the spoon. The second column is not a list of names. It is a list of rooms. *One for them. One for us* — the Warden keeps two ledgers, and the second one is the one that matters. I saw it in the chapel, in the hem of Odile's cassock, folded small as a host.

Do not sleep. The strobe will tell you when.

**Memo from Tobias Renn to Sister Odile, folded into a hymnal, 16 October, 05:41**

Sister —

The intercom in the east stairwell has begun to whine. A high, thin sound, like a wire drawn tight. It comes and goes with the damp. I lost the thread of my count when it started — forty-one, forty-two, and then the whine, and I was somewhere else entirely. If the damping fields are bleeding into the house circuit, the manual posts cannot call one another. We are five rooms and a corridor of silence.

The Warden's office light has been on since 04:00. He is drafting something. I saw the pneumatic tube carry a canister down to the archive at 05:33 — calibration data only, the manifest said, but the canister was too heavy for a single sheet.

Parameter 9.3 — *no guest shall be transferred, isolated, or removed from the register without dual-signature verification and forty-eight hours' notice* — has not been invoked in eleven months. Check the register. Check whether the second ledger has a ninth column.
