# Abschnitt 9: The Calibration Floor

Condensate struck the cable trays below the raised deck. *Plink. Plink. Plink.* A metronome for the thermal drift eating the subfloor insulation. Each drop rewrote the humidity readout on Vera's wrist display—forty-seven percent, forty-eight, forty-nine.

The firmware writer still pulsed: *AWAITING FIVE VOICEPRINTS — CLAUSE 19: EXPRESSION UNBOUNDED*

Tobias wiped solder flux from his thumb. "The virtual fragment holds. But the striker firmware won't burn without the next calibration parameter. Article 26. Education as technical floor."

Oskar didn't look up from his binder. "Elementary, fundamental, compulsory. Technical and professional made generally available. Higher equally accessible on merit. The registry treats it as a data integrity check—proof the crew can maintain the habitat post-reentry."

Mirela turned the foil strip between her fingers. The crease lines caught the discharge lamps, threw a small hard glare across Oskar's face. He blinked. For a moment the salesman's mask slipped—underneath was something tired and counting.

Mirela: "The knockers had no technical training. Ring C was agricultural. Their fragment carried crop-cycle data, not systems knowledge."

Vera: "The parameter doesn't ask for degrees. It asks for *access*."

"Access requires infrastructure," Tobias said. "The module's training banks were wiped during decommission. Zero pedagogical data remains in local storage."

Oskar snapped his binder shut. "Then we fail the integrity check. The firmware refuses the burn. The hatch stays sealed."

A drop hit the tray. *Plink.* Forty-nine percent humidity.

Tobias: "Unless we constitute the training bank ourselves. Four voices. Four domains. We *become* the infrastructure."

Vera's hand hovered over the intercom. "Define the domains."

"Life support. Navigation. Medical. Governance." Tobias counted on fingers smudged with copper. "Each of us teaches one. The others receive. Compulsory. Elementary. Free at the point of delivery."

Mirela: "And the fifth voice? The knockers?"

The lamps stuttered. The ozone smell thickened.

Tobias keyed the firmware writer. A new line appeared: *CALIBRATION 26: PEDAGOGICAL LOOP — AWAITING FOUR TEACHERS, FOUR LEARNERS*
