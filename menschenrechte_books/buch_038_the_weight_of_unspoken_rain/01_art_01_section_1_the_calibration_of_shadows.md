# Section 1: The Calibration of Shadows

Mira Solano's pencil moved across onion-skin paper, recording what the Hush had claimed since dawn. *The water cart's wheel squeak. Old Cass's cough. The number seventeen.* She wrote in the narrow space between her knees, perched on the rusted grating above Verdal's pneumatic tube network. The tubes had seized three days prior—fine paper dust coating the relay contacts like grey frost, each cylinder breathless with undelivered messages. She could feel the vibration of trapped air through her boots, a low pneumatic wheeze that matched the rhythm of her own ribs.

Tomas Vey appeared at the grating's edge, his tin box warm against his sternum. Eleven years of whispers compressed into metal. He did not speak. He never spoke above the threshold the Ministry permitted. Instead he tapped the grating twice—*two*, the signal for *I have something that matters*—and held out a folded square of paper.

Mira took it. The handwriting was not his. It was the Ministry's precise script, printed on water-ration card stock: **CALIBRATION CYCLE 01 — DIGNITY PARAMETER: UNIVERSAL. EQUALITY VECTOR: ACTIVE. CONSCIENCE MODULE: PENDING.** Below, a checkbox remained unmarked: *Brotherhood protocol engaged.*

She read it twice. The fog curled around her ankles, tasting the graphite on her fingers.

"Where did you find this?" she whispered. The word survived—the Hush was thin here, near the cliff's edge where the Listener sat.

Tomas pressed two fingers to his throat, then to the tin box. *Swallowed. Retrieved. Delivered.*

A Ministry drone passed overhead, its underbelly glowing with the Ledger's latest decrees. New rationing. Shadow collection randomized. The drought climbing district by district like a verdict.

Mira slipped the paper into her notebook, beneath the baker's prices. Her pencil hovered over fresh onion skin. She wrote: *The Ministry calibrates dignity. The checkbox for brotherhood remains empty. Tomas Vey brought proof.*

The fog drank the pencil's scratch. But the writing held.

*Chronicle of the Second Dusk: the Ministry issued a calibration order with the brotherhood checkbox unmarked, and Tomas Vey carried it through the seized tubes, and Mira Solano filed it beside the baker's prices.*
