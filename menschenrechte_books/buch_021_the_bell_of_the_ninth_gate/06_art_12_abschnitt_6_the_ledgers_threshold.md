# Abschnitt 6: The Ledger's Threshold

The speaking-tube's brass throat had swallowed the demand whole. Now it breathed back only the slow, patient hiss of a system waiting—its pneumatic artery clogged with the fine, grey flour of pulped ledger-pages, decades of rulings ground to dust by the very pressure that carried them. Tobin wiped a fingertip across the relay contact; it came away silver-grey, conductive as ash.

*Gain reset. Glyph steadied. The clenched hand, the pulsing dot, held within limit.*

He had done this before. The memory sat in his fingers like a worn groove.

"The audit is a threshold," Old Sable said, her voice unraised. "They name it procedure. It is a ban."

The Keeper's hands did not tremble now. They rested flat on the bound volume—its cover scarred by the impressions of every thumb that had pressed it shut. "The ninth gate has no ledger," she said. "Only the margin where a name was scraped away. Only the line they redrew in the night."

Corin shifted, his bound arm a dead weight beneath the table. "The listeners in the stone," he said. "They heard the whine. They heard Tobin's mutter. They heard me name the holding of words." His eyes found Mira. "What else have they held and not given back?"

Mira's palm still burned from the bronze. The hum beneath her hand had deepened, no longer a question but a *calculation*—the bell measuring the space between what was permitted and what was *lived*. "The parameter," she said quietly, "is not the ledger. The parameter is the boundary they drew around a voice. The zone where a word can be detained without writ. Without bell. Without council."

She lifted her hand. The hum did not stop.

"The law protects the space around a life," she said, as if reading from a gauge. "The space where a name belongs to its owner. Where a letter reaches its destination unopened. Where a reputation is not a ledger column to be balanced against water-rations." Her fingers found the speaking-tube's grille, brushing the paper-dust. "They have widened the net. The calibration has drifted. The protection fails when the interference is *authorized*."

Old Sable rose, joints cracking like dry kindling. "Then we do not surrender the ledger. We surrender the *reading* of it."

The Keeper turned the page. The new parameter glimmered, watermark-faint: a boundary-line drawn not in ink but in the pressure of a thumb held too long.

"Third bell," she said. "Two hours. The shaft is still unwatched."

Tobin's screwdriver bit into the relay's adjustment screw. The pneumatic hiss stuttered, cleared—a single clear note threading through the dust.
