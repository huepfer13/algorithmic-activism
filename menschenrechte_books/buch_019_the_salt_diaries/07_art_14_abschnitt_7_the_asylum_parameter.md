# Abschnitt 7: The Asylum Parameter

**April 21**  
The audit notice arrived via the auxiliary vent line at 03:47, printed on thermal stock that curled at the edges before I could flatten it. CENTRAL REGISTRY — SECTOR REVIEW — PRIORITY ALPHA. My fingers left grease smudges on the header. Tomas appeared in the hatch doorway without knocking, his ration card already folded into a sharp rectangle. "They're early," he said. "Three weeks early." The calibrator on the relay is drifting. I noticed it this morning when the pressure gauge flickered between two numbers that shouldn't touch — 0.48 and 0.52, breathing the same space. I logged it anyway. Protocol demands continuity.

**April 22**  
Mira spent six hours re-indexing the water logs, her knuckles white against the binding. Rook's cough has settled into a wet rattle that echoes in the cistern chamber; I allocated him 300ml from the emergency reserve and watched the life-integrity metric climb to 0.93. The privacy parameter (art_12) holds at 0.73, sealed since the manual lock. But the asylum parameter (art_14) — newly instantiated, flagged by the audit schema — sits at 0.00. No inbound requests. No outbound capacity. The field description reads: *capacity to receive external claimants under duress.* I set the calibration threshold at 0.60, the same as life-integrity. Protection of life and physical integrity takes precedence. The system accepted the value without error. That worries me more than a rejection would.

**April 23**  
Old Pavel didn't rise for the morning count. His logbook lies open to a blank page, the nib of his fountain pen dried to a scratch. I found him at the west window, tracing the tide line with a fingertip — three centimeters higher than yesterday. "The island exhales," he murmured. "We're just the dust in its breath." I recorded his caloric intake at zero, water at zero, and flagged the asylum parameter for manual review. The audit team expects full parameter visibility by 08:00 tomorrow. I've accelerated the filing: water logs, calorie ledgers, transmission archives, all stacked in the relay room with fresh checksums. The tube pressure reads 0.51 bar. The calibrator still drifts. The asylum parameter just ticked to 0.01 — a single ghost pulse in the inbound buffer, origin unknown.
