# The Salt Diaries

**March 3**

Rook says I should write things down because the logbook isn't enough anymore. The logbook is for calories. This is for the other thing. I don't know what the other thing is yet.

The lighthouse smells like rust and boiled kelp. Ines has taped the ration charts over the old lamp schematics. Five names in her small square handwriting. Mine at the bottom because I'm newest. Rook says the order doesn't mean anything. Ines says the order means everything and we all know it.

**March 7**

Tomas didn't speak at dinner. Not one word. He just pointed at his bowl when he wanted more and Ines gave him half a ladle and he nodded. Old Pavel talked enough for three people. He talks about the mainland like it's a person he used to know. "They'll come back when the weather turns," he says, and Mira laughs, and it's not a nice laugh.

Mira counts everything. Steps, breaths, the seconds between waves hitting the rocks. She told me yesterday she knows exactly how many days of water we have left. She wouldn't tell me the number.

**March 11**

The tide came higher than the marker. Ines moved the marker. Nobody said anything but we all watched her do it.

Rook and I hauled the last of the salt barrels up from the cellar. He has a bad shoulder and I have two working arms so that's the arrangement. He doesn't thank me. I don't want him to.

**March 14**

Tomas spoke today. One sentence. He said the radio crackled last night around 3 a.m. and then went quiet again. Ines asked him what he heard. He said nothing. Just crackle. Then nothing.

Old Pavel cried a little and pretended he didn't.

**March 19**

Mira found the leak in the cistern. Small. Fixable. She fixed it herself before telling anyone. When Ines asked why she didn't report it immediately, Mira said, "Because I wanted to know if I could."

I don't know what that means. I think Mira does.

**March 22**

Rook says the island is smaller than last month. He paces the perimeter every morning and he says the numbers out loud like a prayer. Today he said them and then he sat down on the rocks and didn't move for an hour.

Ines is rationing words now too. Three sentences per person per meal. She says it's to conserve energy. Tomas said his three sentences were: "This is stupid. We're not saving anything. We're just getting quieter."

Ines didn't punish him. That scared me more than if she had.

**March 25**

I dreamed the mainland answered. In the dream it was a woman's voice and she said, "We know you're there." I woke up and the radio was silent and the tide was already at the second step.

---

**Entry: March 26. Logged by Ines. Water: 41 days at current ration. Food: 29 days. Words: unrestricted until further notice.**
