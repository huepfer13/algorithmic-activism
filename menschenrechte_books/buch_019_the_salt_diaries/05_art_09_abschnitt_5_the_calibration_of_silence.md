# Abschnitt 5: The Calibration of Silence

**April 14**  
The drip has settled into a low, steady thrum against the east stair junction box, the water there still warm from yesterday’s check. I turned the plastic tag over in my palm; the stamped number matches the serial on the blue‑inked paper Mira showed me, a reminder that the machine is still chewing on relics. Tomas muttered about the pipe again—*[ARCHIV-QUERVERWEIS AUS KAPITEL 01]: "The pipe is blocked again. Third time this week."*—and I logged his complaint as a maintenance note, though the words felt thin against the rising tide. The intercom let out a sudden high‑frequency whine, a sharp irritation that snapped my focus mid‑sentence; I jerked my head up, half expecting a voice, but only the hiss of the pneumatic tube answered. I wrote down the new directive: treatment parameter art_05, threshold zero, no exceptions, and added a secondary check—liberty parameter set to article nine, ensuring no arbitrary detention can be triggered by the system. Life integrity stays the primary constraint; if the liberty readout dips below 0.80, the override forces a pause on any ration adjustment until the integrity floor is secured.

**April 15**  
Mira arrived early, her eyes tired but sharp. She pointed to the condensation trails on the wall, noting how they now follow the rhythm of the 47 Hz lamp flicker, as if the building itself is trying to sync with the rising water. I showed her the tag; she traced the number with a fingertip and said, “It’s a key, not just a label.” We recalibrated the treatment parameter again, this time logging a slight increase in the life‑integrity reading to 0.93 after Pavel’s supplemental water ration. The intercom whine returned, shorter this time, a brief sting that made me drop the log sheet; I caught it before it hit the wet floor and noted the timestamp: 02:17. The liberty parameter hovered at 0.82, just above the article‑nine threshold, but the tide’s advance has cut the north marker another two centimeters, and the cistern reads 24. I whispered a promise to myself: we will not let the water decide who gets to speak.

**April 16**  
The drip has stopped. In its place is a low, resonant gurgle from the crawlspace, like the island exhaling. I found the blue paper folded beneath the workbench, the watermark now darker, the serial number faintly glowing under the lamp’s flicker. Tomas entered, silent, and placed a fresh filter on the sump; his hands no longer shake, but his gaze lingers on the water line creeping up the wall. I logged his intake at 1,720 calories, 1.9 liters, life‑integrity at 0.95. The intercom emitted a piercing whine that cut through my thoughts for a heartbeat, then fell away, leaving a silence that felt heavier than before. I opened the maintenance hatch and saw the pipe—still blocked, the same obstruction noted weeks ago—water backing up, pressure building. I wrote a quick note: *If pressure exceeds 1.4 bar, initiate emergency venting; otherwise, maintain current flow.* The liberty parameter now reads 0.79, just under the article‑nine limit, and the system flashes a soft amber. I hesitate, fingers poised over the override, as the tide’s roar grows louder beyond the rusted door.
