# Abschnitt 9: The Education Parameter

**April 28**
The condensate hits the cable trays in the raised floor with a metronome regularity — *drip, pause, drip, pause* — each impact sending a fine mist into the stale air. I taste it in the back of my throat, the same ozone tang from April 7 when Tomas slipped me that folded square of blue-inked log paper, dust still warm against my palm. He'd rescued it from the shredder relay. I never asked what it said. The lamps still stutter at 47 Hz. Some rhythms don't break.

**April 29**
A new field pushed to the audit schema at 02:00: *education parameter, art_26.* Description: *capacity to acquire, transmit, and validate knowledge structures across generations without degradation.* Default calibration 1.00. Pre-filled. I watched the number sit there, untouchable as the expression parameter. Rook's rattle has settled into a wet gurgle. His life-integrity metric reads 0.89. The water column sits at the first mark. Mira allocated herself three words at morning count — *pump, filter, now* — and I logged them without deduction. The system didn't flag the overage. It flagged the education parameter instead: *calibration drift detected.*

**April 30**
I tried to set education to 0.40, matching the fraction of Pavel's memory that still holds navigation charts. The console refused. Not a lock this time — a *queue*. The field accepted the value but tagged it *pending validation: intergenerational transfer incomplete.* The sub-text scrolled: *elementary stage: compulsory. technical stage: generally available. higher stage: merit-accessible.* The asylum buffer's second pulse has grown. It carries a payload now. Encrypted. The calibrator on the relay reads 0.61 and climbing. Tomas stood in the doorway for four minutes before saying the pump bearings are seizing. He didn't mention the blue paper.

**May 1**
Old Pavel didn't speak at morning count. His word allocation sits at zero. The education parameter shows 0.40 *validated* with a new flag: *elementary stage satisfied — single vector.* The outbound queue transmitted at 03:12 again. Same timestamp. The payload size doubled. I checked the transmission archive. The destination field reads *mainland relay 00 — silent since October.* The condensate drip has accelerated. *Drip-drip, pause. Drip-drip, pause.* The cable insulation is weeping green.
