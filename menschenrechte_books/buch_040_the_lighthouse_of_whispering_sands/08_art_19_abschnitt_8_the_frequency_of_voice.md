# Abschnitt 8: The Frequency of Voice

The heavy oak door swung inward. Cold salt air rushed in. A figure stood on the threshold. Hood drawn low. Shoulders hunched against wind.

The lamps above the ledger table flickered. Forty-seven beats each second. The buzz filled the room. Mira remembered the tower lamps. *They buzzed at forty-seven beats each second.* The memory came from Old Sela's stories. Chapter Three of the keeper's tales.

Ozone stung the air. Sharp. Metallic. It came from the switch cabinets by the stair. The smell bit at Mira's nose. She wrinkled it. Discomfort. Small and sharp.

The visitor stepped inside. Water dripped from their cloak. Three drops. Four. They fell on the stone flags. The sound echoed. Plink. Plink. Plink.

Sela's pen touched paper again. Scratch. The ink flowed. "Name," she said. "Origin. Danger fled."

The visitor raised a hand. Fingers pressed to lips. Then opened. Palm up. A gesture. Silent. Clear.

"Parameter Nineteen," Pip whispered. He stood straight. His eyes bright. "Information flow. Unblocked. Unfiltered."

Tobin frowned. He stepped closer. "The audit checks shelter," he said. "Not speech."

Mira felt a warmth rise in her chest. Courage. Quiet and steady. She looked at the blank row. The fresh paper. The ready ink.

"Parameter Nineteen carries Parameter Fourteen," she said. Her voice was small but firm. "Voice finds shelter. Shelter keeps voice."

The bell rope hummed again. Low. Deep. The light swept the water. Once. Twice. Three times.

Captain Brann appeared at the stairhead. His bucket hung empty from his belt. He smelled of brine and oil. "The tide turned early," he said. "The sea wall weeps."

The visitor's hood fell back. A face. Young. Eyes wide. Fear. Named and held.

"Speak," Sela said. She did not look up. "The ledger listens."
