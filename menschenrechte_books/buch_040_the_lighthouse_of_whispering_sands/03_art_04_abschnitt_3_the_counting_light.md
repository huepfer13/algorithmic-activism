# Abschnitt 3: The Counting Light

The lamps in the tower flickered. They buzzed at forty-seven beats each second. A sharp smell of ozone rose from the switch cabinet. It stung the nose.

Mira rubbed her eyes. She remembered the officer. He looked at the rain barrels. He looked at the water marks on the wall. His stamp had fallen soft.

"We count people first," said Sela. Her voice was steady. "Then we count needs."

Pip held a chalk stub. He stood by the sleeping mats. "One for Brann," he said. "One for Sela. One for Tobin. One for me. One for Mira."

Tobin wrote the names in a column. His hand moved slow. "Five sleep places," he said. "Five water shares."

Brann checked the well rope. It was frayed at the knot. "The well gives less each day," he said. "The pump works harder."

A lamp sputtered. The light jumped. Shadows stretched long on the stone.

Mira felt a tightness in her chest. She named it worry. "No one works without rest," she said. "No one carries more than they can."

Sela nodded. She wrote a line under the names. "Rest is recorded," she said. "Rest is a share."

The bell rope hung silent. The tube was still broken. The bell could not speak.

"We remind each other," said Brann. He tapped the ledger. "Every name. Every need. Every limit."

Pip drew a small circle beside his name. "I can carry the small bucket," he said. "Two trips."

"Two trips is your limit," said Sela. She wrote it down. "The record protects you."

The ozone smell grew stronger. The lamps flickered faster.

Mira looked at the empty well mouth. She looked at the rain barrels. One was low. The officer had seen.

"Three days," whispered Tobin. He capped his pen.

The ledger lay open. The next page waited clean.
