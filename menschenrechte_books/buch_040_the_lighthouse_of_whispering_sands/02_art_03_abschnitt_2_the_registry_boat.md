# Abschnitt 2: The Registry Boat

The registry boat came at dawn. It was grey and small and very official.

Mira saw it first from the tower window. She ran down the stairs, two at a time.

"Someone is here," she called. "A boat with a flag."

Old Sela looked up from the ledger. Her pen stopped. "The Central Registry," she said quietly. "They did not send word."

Tobin dropped a bucket. It clanged on the stone floor. "Why are they here?"

"To check our records," said Sela. She closed the ledger and held it tight. "Quickly now. Bring the papers to the table."

Pip peeked around the door. "Are they angry?"

"No one is angry," said Sela. But her hands moved fast. She stacked the loose pages. She smoothed the corners.

Captain Brann came in from the dock. "The registry officer is tying up," he said. "He has a clipboard and a stamp."

Sela spoke low. "We must show every entry. The water counts. The sleep places. The food shares."

Mira remembered the rain barrels. They had been full behind the bell rope. That was two nights ago. "The barrels are still full," she said. "We can show him."

"Good," said Sela. "A full barrel is a clear record."

The officer stepped inside. He wore a grey coat and carried a wooden stamp. "Registry inspection," he said. "I will check the ledger and the stores."

Tobin stood very straight. His hands were shaking a little. "We have everything written down," he said.

The officer opened the ledger. He read the second entry. His stamp hovered above the page.

Sela watched. Brann watched. The bell above them stayed silent.

The officer looked at the rain barrels. He looked at the water marks on the wall. He wrote a number on his clipboard.

"One barrel is low," he said. "Why?"

Mira's heart beat fast. "We carried water for the sick," she said. "Two buckets. That was the rule we made."

The officer paused. His stamp came down. It made a soft, official sound.

"Noted," he said. "The registry will return in three days. Keep the records ready."

He walked back to his boat. The grey flag waved. Then the boat grew small on the water.

Sela sat down slowly. "Three days," she said. "We must check every entry before then."

Brann picked up the ledger. "The tube is still broken," he said. "The bell cannot remind us. So we must remind each other."

Mira looked at the rain barrels. Then she looked at the empty well. "We need a list," she said. "A list of every person and every need."

Pip raised his hand. "I can count the sleep places."

"Then we start now," said Sela. She opened the ledger to a clean page.
