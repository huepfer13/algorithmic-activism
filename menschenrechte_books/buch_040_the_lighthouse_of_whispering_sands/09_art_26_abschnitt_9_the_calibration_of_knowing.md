# Abschnitt 9: The Calibration of Knowing

Drip. Drip. Drip.

The sound came from the subfloor. Cold water hit cable sheaths. A steady rhythm. Like a slow heart.

Mira watched the visitor. Young. Shaking. The hood lay back now. Dark hair stuck to a pale forehead. The gesture from the doorway returned to her mind. *Fingers pressed to lips.* A sign for silence. Or a sign for waiting.

Sela's pen did not move. The ledger page stayed blank. "Parameter Twenty-Six," the old keeper said. Her voice was dry paper. "Calibration required."

Pip tilted his head. "Learning," he said. "Free at the base. Required at the base. Open at the top on merit."

Tobin crossed his arms. "We have no school," he said. "We have the ledger. We have the light."

Captain Brann set his empty bucket down. Clank. "We have the sea rising," he said. "We have salt in the wells."

The visitor's lips parted. A breath. Then words. "I know the desalination cycles. I know the filter matrices. I know the pump harmonics." A pause. "I was taught. Before my island went under."

Mira felt a spark. Warm. Bright. *Knowledge moves.* It does not drown.

"Parameter Twenty-Six links to Parameter Nineteen," Pip whispered. "Voice carries knowing. Knowing builds shelter."

Sela's eyes lifted. Sharp. Kind. "The ledger has no row for teachers," she said. "Only for keepers. For children. For supplies."

"The ledger changes," Mira said. Her hand found the blank row. "We add rows."

A drop fell from the ceiling. Plink. It landed on the visitor's boot. They did not flinch.

"Protection first," Sela said. "Life and body. Then knowing."

"Then teaching," the visitor said. "I can teach. The cycles. The math. The stories."

Tobin nodded once. Slow. "Stories keep us."

The bell rope shuddered. A low note rolled through the stone. The light swept. Once. Twice.
