# Abschnitt 4: The Dripping Cold

The lamps in the tower buzzed at forty-seven beats each second. The sound was steady now. It was almost a song.

Mira stood by the switch cabinet. A drop of water fell from the ceiling. It landed on the cable box with a soft tick. Then another drop. Then another.

"The cold up here makes water," said Tobin. He held his hand under the drip. "The ceiling sweats."

"The cables must stay dry," said Brann. He wiped the box with his sleeve. "Wet cables fail. Failed cables mean no light."

Pip looked up at the ceiling. "Tick, tick, tick," he said. "It sounds like a small clock."

Sela opened the ledger. She drew a line under the last entry. "We add a new rule," she said. "We check the ceiling every hour. We keep the cables dry."

Mira remembered the rain barrels. She remembered the low one. She remembered how the officer had looked at it. She kept that memory close.

"The well gives less," she said. "The ceiling gives water too. But not for drinking."

Tobin nodded. "We catch the drip," he said. "We use it for the lamps. For cleaning. Not for mouths."

Sela wrote this down. "Catch water. Use it well. Keep it apart from drinking water."

Brann climbed the ladder. He placed a small bucket under the drip. The tick became a plink. The plink became a rhythm.

"Good," said Sela. "The rhythm tells us the ceiling is working. We listen for it."

Pip drew a tiny bucket beside his name. "I can empty it," he said. "Two trips."

"Two trips is your limit," said Sela. She wrote it down.

The bucket filled slow. The plink was soft and even.

Mira felt the tightness in her chest again. She named it care. "We watch the cables," she said. "We watch the bucket. We watch each other."

The bell rope hung silent. The tube was still broken.

Tobin capped his pen. He looked at the clean page. "Three days," he whispered. "Then the officer comes back."

Sela closed the ledger. "Then we show him the rules," she said. "Every name. Every need. Every limit."

The drip fell. The lamps hummed. The night was cold and wet and safe.
