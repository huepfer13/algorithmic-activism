# Abschnitt 9: The Calibration Drift

Condensate struck the cable trays in a rhythm that matched the 47 Hz flicker — *drip, pause, drip, pause* — each impact a small percussion against the insulation wrap. Mara tasted copper and mineral rot, the same damp cold that had seeped through the raised flooring since Day Three. The thermal paper in her fingers still carried the warmth of the pneumatic tube's delivery, its edges curling toward the ink like burned leaves.

"Thirty-eight," she said. The number landed flat on the bulkhead. "Retroactive to Day One. That makes every witness who testified above threshold — every witness — retroactively contaminated."

Trent didn't turn. His profile caught the strobe: jaw tight, the tendon in his neck jumping. "The expression parameter was never about speech. It was about *classification*. Forty decibels separated testimony from noise. Thirty-eight reclassifies the first eight days of this hearing into the standard partition. The one the revision team accesses."

"Objection," Okonkwo said. The word lacked its usual weight. The flicker caught his eyes — open, closed, open. "Counsel is testifying."

"Witness is *reading*, Your Honor." Trent's voice carried the same schematic flatness. "Article twenty-six. The consortium's firmware maps educational attainment to a certification channel. Elementary completion: mandatory flag. Technical proficiency: merit-gated access. Higher education: bandwidth allocation tied to consortium loyalty indices." He finally looked at Mara. "The calibration log shows a drift event on Day Six. The certification channel dropped two full tiers. Three million people's children woke up unqualified for the apprenticeship pipeline they'd been tracked into since birth."

Sister Adaeze's binder pressed harder against her chest. The pneumatic tube above the clerk's empty station stayed silent — no dry bark, no cylinder. Mara remembered the relay contacts sparking, spitting paper grit, the tube going dead the morning the clerk finally lifted the cylinder. The same silence now.

"Dr. Halvorsen," Okonkwo said. "The thermal drift in the subfloor. Is it affecting the recording array?"

Halvorsen's fingers moved across his tablet. Condensate beaded on the screen. "The moisture compromises the integrity checksums, Your Honor. Every file written since Day Three carries a latent corruption flag. The consortium's cameras record the corruption. We cannot."

"Then we proceed on the record as it stands," Okonkwo said. "Article twenty-six recalibration. Witness Trent — specify the drift vector."
