# Abschnitt 8: The Expression Threshold

The 47 Hz flicker struck the bulkhead panels in time with Halvorsen's pulse. Ozone pooled thick around the switch cabinets, sharp enough to taste behind the molars. Mara had counted three cycles since the clerk left — each one a strobe-lit snapshot of Okonkwo's face, the woman in grey's fingers tightening on her tablet, Sister Adaeze's binder pressed against her chest like a shield.

"Article nineteen," Trent said from the witness box. His voice carried the flat calibration of someone reading schematics aloud. "The consortium's firmware treats expression variance as a bandwidth allocation. Opinion formation. Information seeking. Cross-border impart. Each mapped to a discrete channel with a hard ceiling. Forty decibels. Anything above registers as signal contamination."

"Forty," Okonkwo repeated. The flicker caught his eyes — open, closed, open. "Not fifty-two."

"Asylum parameter triggers at fifty-two. Expression parameter caps at forty. Twelve decibels of clearance between the right to speak and the right to survive." Trent's gaze found Mara across the deck. "The consortium's engineers didn't just separate them. They made the expression channel a prerequisite. Witness exceeds forty before the asylum partition activates? Their testimony routes to the standard partition. The one the revision team *can* access."

Sister Adaeze stood. "Your Honor, the pneumatic tube —"

Above the clerk's empty station, the pneumatic tube coughed. That same dry, mechanical bark from the first morning. Fell silent.

Mara looked up.

A cylinder sat in the receiving tray. No priority cipher. No registry seal. Just a single sheet of thermal paper, curling at the edges, the ink still warm.
