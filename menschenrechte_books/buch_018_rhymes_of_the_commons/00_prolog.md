# Prologue: Five Lines to Mock the Ledger

When the clerks make the codex too grand,
And decree how each person must stand,
A swift five-line verse
Can dismantle a curse
And return common sense to the land.

Here recorded are verses polite and uncouth,
Where metre and rhythm serve justice and truth.
From the benches of state to the baker's stone floor,
No bureaucrat ever could fasten the door
Against laughter that rhymes with a wink and a roar.
