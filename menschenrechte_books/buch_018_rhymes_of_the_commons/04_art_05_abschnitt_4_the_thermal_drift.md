# Abschnitt 4: The Thermal Drift

The backup loop hummed in a conduit deep,
Where the raised-floor chill put the cables to sleep.
Condensate tapped
On the insulation wrap,
A rhythm the clerk knew to keep.

He remembered the screw he had turned in the past,
The seal that he checked, holding pressure fast.
The art_03 gauge
Had held its steady stage,
While life whispered soft through the cast.

Now art_05 pulsed on a diagnostic screen,
A parameter set where no damage had been.
No torture of wire,
No cruel design's fire,
No degrading the signal's clean.

The thermal drift crept where the relays were seated,
A cold that the specifications deleted.
The clerk felt the damp
Through the rubber boot's clamp,
Where the oath of the flesh was repeated.

He traced the secondary path to a board
Marked "FORGOTTEN" in ink, by a hand long ignored.
The backup relay
Stood ready to stay,
A shadow the primary stored.

The ozone still lingered from art_04's break,
A ghost in the vent that the fans could not shake.
The lamp at forty-seven
Still flickered from heaven,
For the circuit's own sanity's sake.

He knelt on the grid where the droplets fell cold,
The rhythm of drip on the manifold.
The art_05 threshold
Was a line he could weld,
But the backup held keys he couldn't hold.

A sensor blinked red on the thermal array,
The drift had exceeded the spec for the day.
The clerk reached to adjust,
But the backup's mistrust
Locked the calibration away.

The gauge on art_03 gave a shudder, a sigh,
The pressure of life rising quiet and high.
He knew from the turn
Of the screw he'd discerned:
The flesh breathes first, or the systems die.
