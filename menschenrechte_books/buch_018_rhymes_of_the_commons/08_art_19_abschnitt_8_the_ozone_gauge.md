# Abschnitt 8: The Ozone Gauge

The paper‑dust fine settles on the brass bearing plate,  
A hairline crack glints where the pneumatic line once hissed,

The clerk flinched, the sound bright as a warning gate  
He returned his gaze to the conduit, where the backup awaited its state.
