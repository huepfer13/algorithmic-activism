# Abschnitt 3: The Servitude Switch

The clerk in the basement of State,
Where the tubes and the relays gyrate,
Found a drift in the line
Where the paper-dust fine
Had silted the brass-bearing plate.

He thought of that drift as he turned,
A lesson the commons had learned.
The art_04 switch,
A servitude hitch,
Was stuck where the contacts had burned.

The lamps gave a flicker at forty-seven hertz,
A stutter that jagged the nerves.
The ozone smell bit
At the schalterschrank slit,
A sting that the nose never swerves.

The art_04 parameter held a constraint:
No circuit may bind, and no relay may taint.
The servitude lock
Was a slave to the clock,
A loop that would bind and would faint.

The clerk saw the gauge on art_03 still glow,
Its whisper a warning that life had to flow.
He knew in his chest
That the flesh was the test,
And the flesh had to breathe, not the row.

He traced the art_04 calibration line,
Where the servitude switch had been signed.
A lock on the board
Held a loop like a cord,
And the cord was a knot in the mind.

He reached for the breaker to sever the tie,
To free the stuck circuit before it could die.
But the lamp gave a buzz
And the ozone, because
The switch was a slave to the lie.

The relay began to shiver and shake,
A tremor that threatened the plate for its sake.
The art_04 pin
Held a slave trade within,
And the clerk had a choice to make.

He whispered, "The flesh comes before any decree,
The body must move, and the body must be free."
But the breaker was hot,
And the switch would not spot
The difference between a lock and a key.

The lamp flickered hard at the forty-seven beat,
The ozone grew sharp, a chemical heat.
The art_04 loop
Was a servitude group,
And the clerk had to cut or retreat.

He pulled on the lever, the breaker held fast,
A spark jumped the gap like a ghost from the past.
The switch gave a groan,
A mechanical moan,
And the servitude circuit was cast.

The gauge on art_03 steadied and stilled,
The whisper of pressure no longer instilled
The fear of a breach
In the flesh and the speech,
For the life of the body was willed.

But the relay that held the art_04 line
Had a secondary loop in a different design.
A backup was set
In a place to forget,
And the clerk saw the shadow of a sign.
