# Abschnitt 7: The Displacement Gauge

The auditor's notice came down from the tower  
Demanding the logs by the end of the hour  
The clerk felt the sweat on his brow start to bead  
As he rushed through the ledgers with desperate speed  
To file every protocol, parameter, power  

He recalled the pneumatic line's dangerous groan  
Where the paper-dust fine had blocked the brass cone  
The art_12 gauge had flickered a warning of red  
Before the backup relay could fully be fed  
And the pressure had nearly blown seal and tone  

Now art_14 blinked on the panel's front face  
A displacement parameter, seeking a place  
No persecution flag on the incoming stream  
But the calibration drift was a technical dream  
That the audit would catch in its rigid embrace  

The spec sheet read clear: life integrity first  
Before any protocol, parameter, or verse  
The art_14 threshold must hold for the soul  
Seeking a haven, a border, a goal  
Or the system would crash, unrehearsed, unreversed  

He reached for the card with a gloved, trembling hand  
The recalibration order from central command  
But the tube was still blocked by the residue fine  
And the brass-bearing plate showed a hairline design  
That no amount of tapping could make it withstand  

The intercom buzzed with a priority tone  
"Seal art_14 before the audit is shown  
The displacement flag holds but the contact is worn  
The pressure is rising on this frozen morn  
Recalibrate now or the threshold is blown"  

The clerk wrote the value, the stamp pressed down hard  
The formal rhyme entry, the technical card  
But the pneumatic hiss warned of a rupture ahead  
Where the paper-dust fine on the contact had spread  
The art_14 gauge flickered, unguarded, unjarred
