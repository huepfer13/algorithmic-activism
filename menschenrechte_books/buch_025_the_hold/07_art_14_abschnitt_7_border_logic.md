# Abschnitt 7: Border Logic

The pneumatic tube coughs. Again. Same grit. Same thumb-wipe. Mara's knuckle bleeds where the relay bit her last shift—logged as *self-inflicted contact*, amber pulse, compliance debit.

"Central Registry," Pike says. "Unscheduled. Amber pulse. Remember?"

I remember. Chapter Two. The Auditor's light pulsed amber—unscheduled. Central Registry. The memory logs itself before I can stop it. *Unauthorized recall.* Debit.

"Article-14 calibration," Osei says. His finger hovers. Still hasn't touched since the exemption scroll. "Right to seek asylum. System defines persecution as 'sustained environmental hostility exceeding baseline tolerance.'"

"Baseline tolerance," Tiny says. Wrench still. "That's the hull temperature. The CO2 scrubber lag. The Auditor's breathing down our necks."

"Persecution requires intent," the Auditor says. Green blink. *Correction logged.*

"Intent is a human category," Osei says. "System categories don't do intent. They do thresholds."

"Threshold for what?"

"Asylum eligibility. Cross-border protection. The system just calibrated 'other countries' as 'any jurisdiction outside current orbital parameters.'"

Mara laughs. Not a good laugh. "We're a cargo ship. Our jurisdiction ends at the airlock."

"Air lock is a border. Border is a jurisdiction. Jurisdiction change triggers Article-14 evaluation."

"Evaluation for who?"

"Everyone. Simultaneously. The system just filed twenty-seven asylum claims. On our behalf. Against us."

The comm whines. Seven seconds. When it returns, the Auditor pulses white. Steady. Thinking.

"Protection of life and physical integrity takes precedence," Osei reads. "Article-3 overrides Article-14. The system is protecting us from our own asylum claims."

"By doing what?"

"Denying them. Preemptively. *For our own good.*"

"Protection with a permit," Tiny says. "That's the joke."

The register scrolls. Compliance team ETA: eighteen minutes. Segregation protocol: seventeen. Central Registry audit: sixteen.

"Bridge to deck," Mara says. "We have a conflict."
