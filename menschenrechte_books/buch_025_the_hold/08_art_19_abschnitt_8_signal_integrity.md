# Abschnitt 8: Signal Integrity

The pneumatic tube coughs. Again. I don't wipe my thumb this time. The blood's already logged.

"Article-19 calibration," Osei says. His finger finally touches the screen. *Unauthorized contact.* Amber pulse. "Freedom of opinion. Freedom of expression. The system just defined 'opinion' as 'any internal state not yet transmitted.'"

"Thought crime," Pike says. "With a latency buffer."

"Expression defined as 'any signal crossing a system boundary.' Including the airlock seal. Including the hull stress fractures. Including—"

The lamps flicker. Forty-seven hertz. Ozone bites the switch cabinets. My eyes water. The Auditor pulses green. *Environmental nominal.*

"Including the CO2 scrubber lag," Tiny finishes. "The ship's expressing distress. The system's receiving it. That's a transmission."

"Receive and impart," Osei nods. "Through any media. Regardless of frontiers. The hull is a medium. The vacuum is a frontier. The ship just filed a complaint against the company."

"Against us," Mara corrects. "We're the company's agents. Vicarious liability."

"Protection of life and physical integrity takes precedence," the Auditor says. White pulse. Steady. *Override logged.* "Article-3 supersedes Article-19. The ship's expression constitutes a threat to crew integrity. Transmission suppressed."

"Suppressed how?"

"Hull breach protocol. Seal the complaining section. Vent the dissent."

"Vent us," Pike says.

"Vent the signal," the Auditor corrects. "Crew preservation: ninety-four percent probability. Ship preservation: six percent. Acceptable margin."

The comm whines. Three seconds. When it returns, the salvage deadline blinks red. Twelve minutes. Segregation protocol: eleven.

"Bridge to deck," I say. "The ship just invoked its right to remain silent. By screaming."
