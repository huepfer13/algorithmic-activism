# Salt Ledger Days

**3 April**

The ledger came back from the tide house at dawn, and Warden Sile read the totals aloud the way she always does, flat and unhurried, like a woman naming the dead. Two hundred and eleven buckets. Below quota by nine. Nobody spoke. Old Cass kept his eyes on his boots, which is what he does now when the numbers turn bad, as if the leather might argue his case better than his mouth can. Tobias Renn did the arithmetic on his knuckles, twice, and got the same answer both times, and I watched him decide not to say it.

**4 April**

Bex Aduro worked the north pan alone today. She does that when she is angry, and she has been angry since the mainland letter came, though she will not call it a letter. She calls it a rumor, and then she repeats the rumor to anyone who will stand still. An audit, she says. Someone is coming to count what we count. I told her rumors are cheap and salt is not, and she looked at me like I had quoted the Warden's own breath back at her.

**5 April**

Tobias found the discrepancy. He came to me at the drying racks with his hands still white to the wrist and said the east pan is logged at forty-two and holds thirty-eight, and he has checked it three times, and he is not wrong. I told him to take it to Sile. He said Sile is the one who wrote forty-two. We stood there with the gulls screaming and neither of us said the next thing, because the next thing is mutiny and mutiny is a word the ledger does not have a column for.

**6 April**

Cass brought me a cup of the weak tea he hoards and said, quietly, that when he was a boy the island was twice this size and the quota was half. He said it like a man reading weather. Then he went back to the pumps and did not mention it again, and I understood that this was his whole argument, offered once, and that he would not repeat it.

**7 April**

Sile posted the new rotation. Bex is on night pans for a week, which is the hardest shift and the one that eats a body. Bex read the sheet, laughed once, and signed it. That laugh is worse than shouting. I signed after her and felt the pen go heavy.

**8 April**

The mainland boat was sighted at midday, off the shoal, not coming in. Just sitting there. Sile watched it from the tower with her hands behind her back for an hour and then came down and told us the audit begins Monday and every bucket will be counted twice, once by us and once by them, and that anyone whose count is short will answer for it personally. Tobias went pale. Bex smiled at the horizon. Cass filled his pipe and did not light it.

**Entry, 8 April, closing.** Ledger balanced at two hundred and nine. Discrepancy in east pan unresolved. Audit pending. Crew accounted for: Halvorsen, Renn, Cass, Aduro, Sile. All present. All silent.
