[INTRO - CHAIR ON CONCRETE]
(Sub pulse enters, low and slow)
The chair scrapes concrete. Someone sits.
The lamp above the table does not warm.

[INTERROGATION - QUESTION/ANSWER, NO MUSIC]
(No instruments. Room tone only.)
"State your floor."
Twelve.
"State your points."
None.
"State your name."
The paper in the relay says I have none.
(Silence. A shekere shakes once, far off.)

[BROKEN VOICE]
(Close-mic, whispered)
The pneumatic tube coughed up a slip this morning—
fine paper grit on the relay contacts,
the line blocked somewhere between nine and twelve,
and on the slip: a surname with a hyphen,
a foreign shape the scanner could not parse.
So the column read it as a zero.
So the floor read it as a nothing.
Forty families.
One decimal.
(Spring reverb tail)

[ANTI-CHORUS - NUR EIN CELLO]
(Cello alone, bowed slow)
Who signs the form that signs the floor?
Who signs the form?
(Cello holds one note)

[INTERROGATION - REPETITION]
(Talking drum enters, sparse)
"Did you rent to them?"
I rented to the ones whose names came through.
"Did the hyphen change the rent?"
The hyphen changed the rent.
"Did the hyphen change the door?"
The hyphen changed the door.
(Tape hiss rises)
Mauer. Draht. Relais. Nichts.
(Sub pulse returns)

[SYSTEM ERROR - ALL INSTRUMENTS CUT]
(Total silence. Two seconds. Then room tone resumes as if nothing happened.)

[OUTRO - TAPE-STOP]
The Adjuster turns a page.
Marks the time.
(Tape-stop)
The relay clicks—and does not stop clicking.
