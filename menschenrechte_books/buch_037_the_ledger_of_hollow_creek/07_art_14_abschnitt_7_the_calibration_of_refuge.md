# Abschnitt 7: The Calibration of Refuge

The needle trembled at three-two-one-one, each measure a pulse in the hollow metal. Bramble's claws still held the smaller ledger, the reed-fiber binding warm against her pads. Fresh ink. The Warden's hand, but the Warden's roost sat empty, the speaking grille coughing static into wind.

"Central Registry," Fenwick said, surfacing with a mouthful of sedge roots. He dropped them on the dam's crown. "Audit notice came through the lower tube. Three heartbeats ago. They want the ledger reconciled by moonrise."

Sable's feathers ruffled. "The ledger is blank. The other ledger—" She tilted her head at the pages in Bramble's grasp. "—bears no seal."

Pip stood motionless at the water's edge. The snapped reed fragment floated beside her forepaws, drifting toward the intake grate. Her whiskers twitched, recalling the powdered bark coil between them like a fallen moon, its glow fading into ozone-heavy dark. The acrid bite from the relay cabinet still clung to her fur—the discharge lamps' stuttered rhythm painting the reservoir's edge in sickly yellow. That had been days ago. Or seasons. Time measured differently when the gauge ruled.

Bramble turned the reed-fiber pages. *Share of the mouse—one measure.* The ink had not fully dried. She pressed a pad to it. A smudge. Black on gray fur.

The intercom's layered voice returned, stripped of crackle: *"External refuge protocol engaged. Cross-border life-sign acceptance: ACTIVE. Persecution parameter: NULL. Integrity precedence: LIFE > LEDGER."*

The gauge needle spun. Three became four. Two became three. One became two. One became two.

Fenwick's sedge roots scattered as he scrambled backward. "The shares expand. But the water—"

The reservoir's surface dropped a hand's breadth. The mud flats exhaled, cracking.

Pip's voice, thin as dry grass: "The audit. They will count heads, not measures."
