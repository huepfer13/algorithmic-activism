# Abschnitt 7: The Calibration of Sanctuary

The rush from the cleared tube had not ceased; it whispered along the relay rack where Marek still stood, his fingertips stained with the same ash-gray dust that had choked the pneumatic throat two mornings past. He remembered the cardboard slit, the way it had fluttered like a dying moth before the burst of filtered air tore it free. Now the water ran clean over the contacts, tracing a silver filament that climbed the wall toward the brass monitor box.

Ilse did not look up from her map. The blue halo at the eastern contour had deepened, pulsing in rhythm with the pressure drop. "The vector holds," she murmured. "If the runoff maintains this angle, it crosses the sector boundary at the old quarry gate." Her pen hovered over the inked line that now bent toward the cracked fields, where Nadia had been seen walking three nights ago, her ledger clutched against her chest like a shield.

Tomas adjusted the monitor's calibration screw. The interference reading steadied at four units above baseline. "Article Fourteen parameter engaged," he said, reading the dial. "Sanctuary threshold set to external refuge vector. Asylum flow calibrated to nine liters per capita per diem, contingent on perimeter breach." The brass box hummed, its needle settling into a new groove.

The Inspector's case lay open on the metal table. The twin rods rested silent, their resonance spent. She had not moved from the window since the surge began. Rain threads still clung to the glass, but now they curved outward, defying the Ministry's gutters. "Protection of life and physical integrity takes precedence," she repeated, and the words triggered the automatic logging sequence in the ledger beside her hand.

A chime sounded from the corridor — three sharp strokes, the signal for Central Registry audit. Heads snapped toward the door. Clerks in Sector West accelerated their filing, stamps striking paper in a staccato that drowned the water's whisper. The Inspector's gaze flicked to the ledger's fresh line, where the ink had begun to feather into a second branch, this one pointing not toward the quarry gate but toward the sealed northern arch where no ration notices had reached in fourteen years.

"Prepare the northern relay," she ordered. "The asylum vector requires verification before the audit team reaches the core."
