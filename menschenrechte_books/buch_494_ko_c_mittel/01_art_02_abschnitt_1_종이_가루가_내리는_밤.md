[INTRO - SOUND SYSTEM]

[Spoken Word - Heavy Delay]

"이게 다 어디서 막힌 거야."

[VERSE 2 - CLOSE MIC]
[Whispered Vocals]

"이 도시는 숨을 못 쉬는구나."

[BRIDGE - PSYCHEDELIC]
[Instrumental Chorus]

[VERSE 3 - SPOKEN WORD]
[Spoken Word - Heavy Delay]
